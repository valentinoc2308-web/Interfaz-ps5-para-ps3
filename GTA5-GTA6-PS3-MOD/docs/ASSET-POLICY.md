# Asset and licensing policy

Allowed:
- Original assets created for this project.
- Assets with a license that explicitly permits the intended modification and redistribution.
- User-provided files used locally for personal testing, when lawful; do not commit copyrighted game files.

Not allowed in this repository:
- Extracted GTA VI/GTA V proprietary models, textures, maps, audio, missions, or source code.
- Full game data, disc images, PKG files, RAP/license files, encryption keys, or title updates.
- Assets copied from other mods without permission and license review.

Every non-original asset must have a record of its author, source, license, required attribution, and permitted redistribution. If that cannot be verified, it does not go into a public release.
